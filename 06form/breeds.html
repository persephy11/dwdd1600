Guinea Pig Breed Varieties

Short Haired Breeds

Short haired varieties are the most resilient and beginner-friendly companions in the cavy world. Their smooth coats require minimal grooming compared to long-haired breeds, making them ideal for everyday handling. Breeds like the American boast a flat, glossy coat, while the Abyssinian is famous for its symmetrically arranged cowlicks known as rosettes.

Long Haired Breeds

Long haired cavies, such as the Peruvian, Silkie, and Coronet, feature sweeping locks that require dedicated daily maintenance. Their coats can grow several inches in length, requiring gentle daily brushing, occasional trimming, and extra cage hygiene to prevent tangles and mats. While they demand more time, their luxurious appearance makes them an exceptionally stunning presence in any home.

Hairless Breeds

Hairless cavies include the Skinny Pig (which has small tufts of fuzz on its nose and feet) and the completely hairless Baldwin. Without a natural fur coat, these unique cavies require ambient room temperatures between 70°F and 75°F, specialized fleece bedding that protects their delicate skin from scrapes, and slightly higher caloric intake to maintain normal body temperature.

Popular Breed Lineup

  • American: Sleek, smooth-coated classic short haired breed.
  • Abyssinian: Short coat featuring distinct, swirling whorls (rosettes).
  • Peruvian: Regal long coat with natural hair forward-parting over the head.
  • Silkie: Flowing long hair that cascades smoothly straight back from the nose.
  • Skinny Pig: Mostly hairless body with crinkled whiskers and muzzle fuzz.

Coat Care & Handling Steps

  1. Gently support both the chest and hindquarters with two hands whenever lifting your pet.
  2. Brush coats in the natural direction of growth to clear loose debris and shedding hair.
  3. Inspect ear flaps, foot pads, and coat condition during every grooming routine.

Breed Classification Terms

Rosette
A circular whorl or cowlick of fur radiating evenly from a central pinhead.
Crest
A single crown-like swirl positioned squarely in the middle of the forehead.
Agouti
A natural coat color pattern where individual hair strands feature alternating bands of dark and light pigment.
