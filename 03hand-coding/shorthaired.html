Short Haired Guinea Pig Breeds

Overview of Short-Coated Cavies

Short haired guinea pigs are among the most popular and beginner-friendly companions in the cavy fancy. Their short coats require significantly less daily brushing than long-haired varieties, making them ideal for first-time owners and families with young children. A weekly brush using a soft bristle brush is typically all that is needed to minimize shedding and keep their skin healthy.

Despite their shared low-maintenance coats, short-haired varieties display an impressive array of textures, cowlicks, and color patterns. Whether you are admiring the silky smoothness of an American cavy or the dense, springy coat of a Teddy, each breed possesses a distinct look paired with an exceptionally charming personality.

Popular Short-Coated Varieties

  • American: The most common variety worldwide, recognized by a smooth, sleek coat that lies flat against the body.
  • Abyssinian: A spirited breed characterized by structured cowlicks known as rosettes arranged evenly across the body.
  • Teddy: Named for its resemblance to a plush toy, this cavy features a short, dense, and kinked coat that stands upright.
  • American Crested: Similar to the smooth American cavy, but marked by a single contrasting white rosette on the forehead.

Grooming and Handling Routine

  1. Gently support the chest and hindquarters with both hands whenever picking up your cavy.
  2. Use a soft-bristle brush to remove loose hair and debris along the natural direction of coat growth.
  3. Inspect ears, eyes, and nails during every grooming session to monitor overall health.

Breed Classification Terms

Rosette
A circular whorl or cowlick of hair radiating out from a distinct center point.
Crest
A single crown-like swirl located directly atop the guinea pig's forehead.
Agouti
A natural wild-type coloration where each individual hair shaft displays alternating bands of light and dark pigment.
