Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include:

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; famous for his unusually large signature.
Thomas Jefferson
Primary author of the Declaration; later became the 3rd U.S. president.
Benjamin Franklin
Scientist, inventor, diplomat, and one of the most prominent Founding Fathers.
John Adams
Major advocate for independence; later became the 2nd U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Revolutionary leader and influential organizer in Massachusetts; cousin of John Adams.
George Washington
Commander of the Continental Army and later the 1st U.S. president. He did not sign the Declaration.
John Witherspoon
President of the College of New Jersey (now Princeton) and the only active college president to sign.
Robert Morris
Wealthy Philadelphia merchant who played a major role in financing the Revolution.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
The only Catholic signer and one of the last surviving signers.
George Walton
Georgia delegate who later served as governor of Georgia.

Each of these individuals played a significant role in the founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in signing the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by the British Crown.
